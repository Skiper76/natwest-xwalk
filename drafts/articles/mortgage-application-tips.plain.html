What to prepare before your mortgage application

James Okafor
3 August 2026

A little preparation goes a long way toward a smoother, faster mortgage application.

Lenders look at more than just your income. Here's what to have ready before you apply.

Proof of income

Three to six months of payslips, or two years of accounts if you're self-employed.

Bank statements

Recent statements help us understand your spending patterns and regular commitments.

Details of your deposit

Be ready to show where your deposit is coming from, whether it's savings, a gift, or an inheritance.

A realistic budget

Know your monthly limit before you start viewing properties, it keeps the search focused.
