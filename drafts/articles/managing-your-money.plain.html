Five simple ways to manage your money better

Practical, no-nonsense tips to help you build better financial habits, starting today.

Managing money well doesn't require a finance degree, just a few consistent habits repeated over time.

1. Track where it goes

Before you can change your spending, you need to see it clearly. Use the NatWest app to review your last three months of transactions and group them by category.

2. Automate your savings

Set up a standing order that moves money into a savings account the day you get paid, so saving happens before you have a chance to spend it.

3. Build a small emergency fund

Even a few hundred pounds set aside can stop an unexpected bill from becoming a crisis.

4. Review your subscriptions

Recurring payments add up quietly. A quarterly check-in can uncover services you no longer use.

5. Set one clear goal

Whether it's a deposit for a first home or a holiday fund, a specific target makes it easier to stay motivated.
